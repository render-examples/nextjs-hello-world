# Climate Hack 2022 — 14th Place Solution

Placed **14th out of ~130 teams** with a score of **0.76036** in the Climate Hack 2022 satellite imagery prediction challenge.

[Climate Hack Website](https://climatehack.ai)

---

## The Problem

Predict **24 frames** (2 hours) of satellite weather imagery from **12 input frames** (1 hour of history). Images at 12 frames/hour — effectively video prediction for weather satellite imagery.

---

## Rationale: Why These Architectures?

I tried a progression of architectures, learning what works (and what doesn't) for spatiotemporal video prediction. Time was tight — I learned about this hackathon VERY late — so I iterated fast. Essentially anything that could handle any sort of sequential image data was viable.
I was led astray by the organizers into trying attention-based models — big mistake!. I didn't learn how hard these models are to train until well into development.

---

## Architectures I Tried

### 1. Basic Dense Model — *Absolute Garbage* 🗑️

Just a few dense layers with sigmoid output and a scaling factor (the default used ReLU which made no sense for bounded imagery).

**Score: ~0.61** — baseline only.

Okay honestly, this score isn't that bad considering how simple the model is. I remember that changing the output to use a sigmoid (0-1 range) and scaling to the correct output space resulted in such a dramatic change over the ReLU default that was given to us. I ended up using this output style for every architecture I tried afterward because of how successful it was.

---

### 2. Attention-Based Approaches

| Approach | Result | Notes |
|----------|--------|-------|
| **Perceiver / Perceiver IO** | ❌ Failed | Couldn't get working on a dummy classification problem, let alone this spatio-temporal problem. Discovered LAMB optimizer later — might have helped with large batch sizes. |
| **Transformers + WGAN-GP Encoder/Decoder** | ❌ Abandoned | Ambitious: WGAN-GP encoder → latent space → transformer for temporal prediction. Encoder/decoder shared per frame. Similar to Perceiver's latent transformer. Abandoned when VRAM exploded with my setup. |

---

### 3. CNN-Based Approaches

#### Stupid Simple Conv
Basic convolutions → convolutional transpose encoder-decoder. No skip connections.

**Score: ~0.68** — surprisingly decent for a "brain-dead" baseline.

---

### UNets — The Winner 🏆

UNet architecture with staged downsampling/upsampling and **skip connections** between corresponding encoder/decoder layers. This enables information flow across scales — critical for preserving spatial details in video prediction.

| Variant | Score | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| **My Original UNet** (adapted) | ~0.74 | 2nd place (among my own  models) — adapted standard UNet for spatiotemporal prediction |
| **UNetDeep** | **0.76036** 🏆 | **Best score** — deeper convolutions in bottleneck/latent section |

> **Why UNets won:** Skip connections preserve high-frequency spatial details that pure encoder-decoder loses. The deeper bottleneck in UNetDeep captures more abstract temporal patterns without losing spatial fidelity.

---

## Key Technical Decisions

### 1. **LAMB Optimizer**
Discovered mid-hackathon. Enables **large batch sizes with higher learning rates** — critical for transformer/Perceiver architectures I couldn't make work in time. Would revisit with LAMB.

### 2. **Skip Connections Are Non-Negotiable**
The jump from Simple Conv (0.68) → UNet (0.74+) proves skip connections are essential for preserving spatial structure in video prediction.

### 3. **UNetDeep Bottleneck**
Deeper convolutions in the latent space capture more abstract temporal dynamics without losing spatial resolution — the key to 0.76036.

### 4. **No N-Body / Attention in Time**
The winning model uses pure CNN with skip connections. Attention/Transformers need LAMB + more VRAM + time I didn't have.

## What I'd Do Differently

1. **Start simple, iterate** — I had the right idea with my *Basic Dense Model* baseline model. I should have branched to the obvious choice of adding convolutional layers before trying any fancy architectures.
2. **Just use frameworks man** — I was doing a lot of hyperparameter tuning manually. I should have simply delegated to a framework like RayTune.
